## 1. Context & Triggers

- Orders occasionally and non-habitually; QC grocery is not yet part of a weekly routine. [evidence] — Source: #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen); #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal)
- 76% of users know the specific item they want before opening the app — NTQC arrives with a predefined shopping list, not open-ended browsing intent. [evidence] — Source: #324 QC Search E2E, Desk Research (Tijana Mesterovic)
- Orders are triggered by a specific top-up need (missing ingredient, last-minute restock) rather than planned bulk grocery runs. [evidence] — Source: #324 QC Search E2E, Synthesis Report (Touhid Kamal)
- 80% of last orders were placed within 1–3 hours of the felt need — urgency is a secondary driver even for NTQC, though less acute than Active QC. [evidence] — Source: #324 QC Search E2E, Desk Research (Tijana Mesterovic)
- Typically orders for the household, not just themselves. [assumption — no NTQC-specific data on order-for context]

**Assumption %**: 20%

## 2. Goals & Motivations

- Primary goal when opening the app: find a single shop that stocks all needed list items at an acceptable price, ideally with a deal. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal); P12 (CZ, NTQC)
- "Successful order" means completing the basket without needing to split across multiple shops or make a second order. [evidence] — Source: #324 QC Search E2E, Synthesis Report (Touhid Kamal) — "New users hitting a wall"
- Primary emotional driver: avoiding a physical store trip — convenience is the entry point, but it is conditional on price being comparable enough to justify the delivery cost. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal); #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen)
- 31% of QC users (skewed toward lower-frequency/newer users) still prefer offline channels for grocery inspiration and social discovery. [evidence] — Source: #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen)
- Constant mental calculation around delivery fees, small-order fees, and MOV — cost control is a primary motivator, not just a hygiene factor. [evidence] — Source: #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen); #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal)

**Assumption %**: 0%

## 3. Mental Model & Behaviours

- Navigates by browsing categories, not the search bar, for grocery — confirmed 8 of 12 participants in #379 bypassed search entirely for QC grocery tasks. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal)
- Mentally associates the app search bar with restaurant food delivery ("where do I find the burger place?"), not with grocery item lookup. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal); P12 (CZ, NTQC): *"I would never type 'milk' into the search — that feels like I'm looking for a restaurant"*
- Arrives with an e-commerce multi-cart mental model (built basket across Amazon, Lazada, Zalora): expects to add items from multiple shops into one unified cart. [evidence] — Source: #324 QC Search E2E, Synthesis Report (Touhid Kamal)
- When the single-shop constraint surfaces (cannot mix carts), NTQC users experience confusion, stall, and frequently abandon the task rather than restructuring their basket. [evidence] — Source: #324 QC Search E2E, Synthesis Report (Touhid Kamal) — "single-shop constraint is a blocker for new users"
- Decisiveness profile is Low to Mid: brand-agnostic for most grocery items, open to platform deal nudges, will switch shop if the anchor item is available at lower price with comparable delivery terms. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Analysis Doc (Touhid Kamal); confirmed across 12/12 participants for decisiveness dimension
- 85% of users search for specific items rather than specific shops — NTQC is item-first, shop-follows. [evidence] — Source: #324 QC Search E2E, Desk Research (Tijana Mesterovic)

**Assumption %**: 0%

## 4. Pain Points

- Single-shop constraint violates the multi-cart mental model and is the primary structural blocker: NTQC cannot complete a mixed-item basket across shops without re-planning from scratch. [evidence] — Source: #324 QC Search E2E, Synthesis Report (Touhid Kamal)
- 40% of NTQC-proximate users blocked by affordability: price markups vs. supermarket, compounded by delivery fees and MOV thresholds, create a value gap that prevents conversion. [evidence] — Source: #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen)
- 20% blocked by quality and trust, concentrated in fresh produce: fresh trust score 4.0/7 — significantly lower than platform average and below the threshold NTQC needs to commit. [evidence] — Source: #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen); #380 Fresh & Assortment Research Plan (Sofia Pevganen)
- Discovery friction: category browsing is the NTQC default but the category taxonomy does not match a physical supermarket layout, creating disorientation. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal)
- Price markup vs. physical supermarket is perceived as high even when objectively comparable — the perceived value gap is as damaging as the actual price gap. [evidence] — Source: #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen); #379 Item First QC Experience, P12 (CZ) (Touhid Kamal)

**Assumption %**: 0%

## 5. Jobs-to-be-Done

- **Functional JTBD 1**: When I realise I'm missing a key ingredient or household item, I want to find everything I need in one shop so I can complete one order without a second trip or second order. [evidence] — Source: #324 QC Search E2E, Synthesis Report (Touhid Kamal); #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal)
- **Functional JTBD 2**: When I'm doing a grocery top-up, I want to avoid going to a physical store so I can save the time and effort of travelling and queueing. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal); #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen)
- **Functional JTBD 3**: When I pick a shop, I want to know upfront whether my full list is available so I don't have to abandon and restart my basket. [evidence] — Source: #324 QC Search E2E, Synthesis Report (Touhid Kamal)
- **Emotional JTBD**: I want to feel confident that what arrives matches what I ordered — especially for fresh items — and that I wasn't overcharged compared to the supermarket. [evidence] — Source: #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen); #379 Item First QC Experience, P1 (CZ) (Touhid Kamal)

**Assumption %**: 0%

## 6. Shop Selection Signals

- The most expensive item in the basket anchors shop choice: NTQC selects the shop where the anchor item is available at the best price, then fills remaining items from that same shop. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Analysis Doc (Touhid Kamal) — confirmed as dominant selection logic across 12/12 sessions
- Shop selection priority order: price of anchor item → delivery fee → MOV threshold → delivery time → shop trust. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Analysis Doc (Touhid Kamal)
- A significant deal (voucher, percentage discount, or zero delivery fee) on the anchor item will override any nascent shop preference. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal); #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen)
- PandaMart / Foodora Market not yet established as a trusted default: NTQC has insufficient experience to distinguish first-party from third-party shops and does not prioritise platform-owned stores. [assumption — no NTQC-specific first-party trust data in indexed sources]

**Assumption %**: 25%

## 7. Trust & Risk Tolerance

- Fresh produce trust is significantly lower than for packaged goods: 4.0/7 for NTQC-proximate users — the "I can't see it before I buy it" concern is unresolved. [evidence] — Source: #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen); #380 Fresh & Assortment Research Plan (Sofia Pevganen)
- NTQC checks shop reviews and ratings before committing to an unfamiliar vendor — social proof is a low-effort trust proxy. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal)
- Price discrepancy between app listing and perceived market price (supermarket mental benchmark) actively breaks trust and triggers session abandonment. [evidence] — Source: #317 QC Problem Sizing Survey (Sofia Pevganen); #379 Item First QC Experience, P12 (CZ) (Touhid Kamal): *"If the milk costs that much, I'll just go to Billa"*
- Low overall risk tolerance: prefers well-known branded items over QC-exclusive or unbranded alternatives; avoids experimenting with unknown SKUs on a first order. [evidence] — Source: #379 Item First QC Experience, Full Deck (Touhid Kamal); decisiveness profile analysis

**Assumption %**: 0%

## 8. Retention & Habit Signals

- No Pro subscription — definitionally new to the platform; subscription value proposition is not yet legible. [assumption — definitional for NTQC; no session data on Pro consideration for first-timers]
- Physical store remains the active fallback: 93% of QC Fresh users are in a trial phase (not habitual), and for NTQC, one bad experience (wrong item, quality failure, late delivery) is likely to reactivate the physical store default. [evidence] — Source: #380 Fresh & Assortment Research Plan (Sofia Pevganen) — 93% trial-phase stat
- Reorder and "order again" behaviour is low frequency; habit formation requires at least one frictionless end-to-end experience to anchor a second order. [assumption — no NTQC-specific reorder data in indexed sources]
- Churn to competitor apps is less likely than churn to physical stores for NTQC; they haven't yet invested in app-switching behaviour. [assumption — inferred from #317 offline preference data; no direct competitor switch data for NTQC]

**Assumption %**: 75%

## What would strengthen this archetype

- **Retention and reorder data**: No NTQC-specific data on second-order triggers, "order again" usage, or what converts a trial user to a light-habitual QC user. A dedicated NTQC reorder study or cohort analysis would fill this gap.
- **EU market representation**: #379 qualitative data comes from CZ, HU, SG, HK — NTQC behaviour in MENA or LATAM markets (foodpanda's high-growth regions) is not represented.
- **Pro subscription consideration for new users**: No data on whether NTQC users encounter or consider Pro during onboarding; the subscription's role in reducing perceived affordability barriers for NTQC is entirely unmeasured.
- **PandaMart / Foodora Market first-party trust**: Shop Selection Signals rely on general shop selection logic; no NTQC-specific data on awareness or trust of platform-owned storefronts.
- **Competitor switching dynamics**: #317 captures offline-to-QC intent but does not distinguish churn to competitor apps (GrabMart, Getir, Gorillas) from churn back to physical stores. NTQC churn routing is unknown.
- **#315 QC In-app Project**: File was inaccessible during archetype generation — revisit once access is confirmed.
